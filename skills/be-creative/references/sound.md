# Sound

Read this reference together with [time.md](time.md).

## Direction

Cover the instrumentation, timbre, harmony, dynamics, and any relevant voice or ambience.

## Refinement

Refine in this order:

1. You **MUST** refine the mix balance.
2. You **MUST** refine the loudness.

## Accessibility

1. You **MUST** keep speech intelligible.
2. You **MUST** keep loudness comfortable and avoid listening fatigue.
3. You **MUST NOT** rely on sound alone to convey important information.
