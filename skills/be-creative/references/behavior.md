# Behavior

## Refinement

You **MUST** keep the experience continuous, so it feels like an extension of the person rather than a sequence of separate states.

1. You **MUST** respond to every input immediately.
2. You **MUST** give continuous feedback during an interaction rather than only at its end.
3. You **MUST** let people interrupt and redirect any motion or sound at any moment. Continue from its current position, velocity, or level instead of jumping or restarting.
4. You **MUST** keep a consistent sense of space. Let things leave and return along the same path, and let things that look the same behave the same across screens and devices.
5. You **MUST** design the visuals, motion, sound, and interaction together, so no one can tell where one ends and another begins.
