# Time

## Direction

Cover the tempo, rhythm, pacing, and the character of any motion or transitions.

## Refinement

Refine in this order:

1. You **MUST** refine the rhythm and pacing.
2. You **MUST** refine the timing and transitions.
3. You **MUST** keep continuity, so the artifact feels like one unbroken experience rather than a sequence of separate states.
   - Carry recurring elements, such as a color, a shape, a motif, or a sound, across parts such as screens, scenes, or sections, and connect the parts with deliberate transitions.
   - Shape melodic lines and motion paths as continuous contours within a phrase, so the ear or the eye follows them naturally.
4. You **MUST** deviate from exact timing on purpose when it improves expression. For example, place notes or motion slightly ahead of or behind the beat to shape the phrasing and emotion.
