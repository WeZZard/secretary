# Audiovisual

Read this reference together with [visual.md](visual.md), [time.md](time.md), and [sound.md](sound.md).

## Refinement

1. You **MUST** choose on purpose the points where sound and visuals align. Sound and visuals that occur at the same moment fuse into one event for the audience, even when they are unrelated in reality.
2. You **MUST** decide on purpose when the sound leads or trails the visuals.
