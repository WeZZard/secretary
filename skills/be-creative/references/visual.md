# Visual

## Direction

Cover the color scheme, layout, typography, spacing, and any relevant imagery.

## Refinement

Refine in this order:

1. You **MUST** establish the visual hierarchy, so the most important element is the most obvious one.
2. You **MUST** refine the proportions, spacing, and alignment.
3. You **MUST** refine the contrast.
4. You **MUST** arrange related elements along continuous lines or curves within a frame, so the eye follows them naturally.
5. You **MUST** adjust shapes optically rather than mathematically when exact geometry looks wrong.

## Accessibility

1. You **MUST** keep text readable.
2. You **MUST NOT** rely on color alone to convey important information.
