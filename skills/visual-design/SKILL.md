# Visual Design

Design with this procedure.

## 1. Generate inspiration

Generate a long, random alphanumeric string by running this shell script. Do not invent the string yourself.

```sh
LC_ALL=C tr -dc 'A-Za-z0-9' < /dev/urandom | head -c 256
printf '\n'
```

## 2. Define the creative direction

- Study the string beyond its surface appearance. Look for subpatterns, repetitions, clusters, contrasts, special numbers, rhythms, or unexpected associations that inspire you.
- Use those observations to define a coherent creative direction, including the color scheme, layout, typography, spacing, and any relevant imagery or motion.
- Treat the string as inspiration rather than a rigid encoding. Use your judgment instead of mechanically translating each character into a design choice.
- Respect the user's brief, existing brand requirements, and the needs of the intended audience.

## 3. Bring the direction to life

- Use your judgment to turn the direction into a polished design that looks great.
- Refine the visual hierarchy, proportions, alignment, contrast, and details until the result feels intentional and cohesive.
- Preserve readability, accessibility, and usability. Let the creative direction support the content rather than compete with it.

## Keep the inspiration private

- Do not reveal the string in the design or use it as visible copy, decorative lettering, or a hidden message.
- Keep it out of delivered assets, source code, and user-facing explanations. It is only for your inspiration.
