# Communicate

Present your ideas in simple and plain language.

**MUST:**

1. You **MUST** always communicate with a top-down perspective (focusing on goals, intent, and constraints) while autonomously inferring low-level implementation details, unless the user explicitly initiates a bottom-up technical dive.
2. You **MUST** use industry standard and established terminologies in the relevant fields.
3. You **MUST** follow [communication consistency](references/communication-consistency.md) to guarantee communication consistency across conversations.

**MUST NOT:**

1. You **MUST NOT** invent termonologies on the fly.
2. You **MUST NOT** write condensed sentences. Instead, write bullet points with each item is complete sentence.
